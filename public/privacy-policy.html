Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 September 2026

GRIND is a private, invite-only training journal. This page explains what it stores, why, and how to remove it.

What GRIND stores

  • Your account: email address and display name.
  • What you log: workouts, lifts, notes, meals, supplements and any photos you add.
  • Fitbit data, only if you connect it: workouts, sleep, heart rate, steps and body measurements. Access is read-only.
  • Notification settings, only if you turn them on: your device's push address and time zone.

How it is used

Only to run the app for you: showing your history and progress, and sending the notifications you switched on. There are no ads and no analytics, and your data is never sold.

Google and Fitbit data

GRIND's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can disconnect Fitbit at any time in Settings, or from your Google Account.

Who else can see it

Nobody else. Your data is stored with Supabase, and the site is hosted by Vercel. If you turn on notifications, your device's push service (Apple, Google or Mozilla) delivers them.

Deleting your data

You can export everything from Settings → Data. To delete your account and all of its data, email the address below.
